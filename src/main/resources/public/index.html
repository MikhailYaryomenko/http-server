David Goggins

David Goggins is a badass because he transformed himself from a depressed, overweight exterminator into a U.S. Navy SEAL, Army Ranger School graduate, and one of the world’s top ultramarathon runners through sheer, relentless discipline. He thrives on embracing the “40% Rule,” pushing past the point where most people quit to prove that human limits are largely self-imposed. Goggins once completed over 4,000 pull-ups in 17 hours to raise money for military charities, tearing his hands open but refusing to stop until the goal was crushed. Ultimately, his unfiltered, no-excuses mentality and willingness to suffer for greatness embody the very definition of badassery.
